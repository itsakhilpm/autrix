Laboratory Tubes

Sample Storage
Product Overview

Reliable Containment from Bench to Centrifuge

AURTIX laboratory tubes are built for dependable sample containment through every stage of your workflow. Moulded from optically clear, chemical-resistant polypropylene, they withstand high-speed centrifugation, temperature extremes, and aggressive reagents — ideal for diagnostics, research, and specimen processing.

  • Rated for high-speed centrifugation without deformation
  • Leak-proof caps with positive-seal design
  • Crisp printed graduations and frosted writing area
  • Withstands freezing and autoclave sterilisation

Key Features

Built for Demanding Workflows

Centrifuge Ready

Uniform wall thickness and reinforced conical bases handle high RCF spins run after run.

Temperature Resilient

Stable from deep-freeze storage to autoclave cycles, protecting samples across extreme conditions.

Leak-Proof Seal

Precision-threaded caps create a positive seal that keeps samples secure during transport and mixing.

Optical Clarity

Crystal-clear walls and sharp graduations make visual inspection and volume checks effortless.
