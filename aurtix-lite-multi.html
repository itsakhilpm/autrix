Product Description

Built for Plate-Based Workflows

The Aurtix Lite Multi-Channel (catalogue name: TopPette Mechanical Pipette) extends Aurtix Lite's accessible, fully mechanical design across 8 or 12 channels, letting labs handle 96- and 384-well plate work at the same reliable, budget-friendly tier as the single-channel model. A shared volume dial adjusts every channel in unison, so there's no risk of channel-to-channel drift from inconsistent manual settings.

The evenly balanced tip-ejection mechanism and consistent channel spacing keep alignment true across the plate, reducing the mis-seating and cross-contamination risk that comes with worn or uneven multichannel pipettes. Like the rest of the Lite range, it's built around an autoclavable lower assembly for straightforward decontamination between protocols.

Aurtix Lite Multi-Channel is the pipette we recommend for labs moving into plate-based assays — ELISA, cell-based screening, PCR set-up — without yet needing an electronic or premium instrument.
