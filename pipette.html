Product Overview

Accuracy You Can Feel in Every Dispense

AUTRIX pipettes are engineered for exceptional accuracy and ergonomic comfort. A lightweight body, smooth plunger action, and a clearly visible volume display make them ideal for long sessions in clinical, diagnostic, and research laboratories — delivering consistent, repeatable results every time.

  • Fully autoclavable lower assembly for easy decontamination
  • Precise volume adjustment with secure click-stop lock
  • Ergonomic grip designed to reduce hand fatigue
  • Universal compatibility with AUTRIX and standard tips
