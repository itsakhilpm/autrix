Product Description

Engineered for Purity

Aurtix Tips are produced under strict cleanroom conditions to guarantee purity from the production line to the pipette. Every tip is moulded from high-purity virgin polypropylene — never recycled resin — so labs get consistent wall thickness, a true universal cone fit, and none of the leachable contaminants that can compromise sensitive assays.

Filtered variants add a hydrophobic aerosol barrier that stops liquids and aerosols from ever reaching the pipette shaft, protecting the instrument and preventing cross-contamination between samples — essential for PCR, qPCR, and other trace-sensitive work. The low-retention tip surface releases the full programmed volume even with viscous, foaming, or precious samples, improving accuracy run after run.

Aurtix Tips seat securely on Aurtix pipettes and all major third-party brands with no wobble or leaks, and ship racked, in refill trays, or in bulk bags depending on your lab's workflow and sterility requirements.
