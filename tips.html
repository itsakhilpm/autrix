Pipette Tips

Consumables
Product Overview

Contamination-Free Transfer, Every Time

Manufactured from high-purity virgin polypropylene in a cleanroom environment, AUTRIX pipette tips deliver a secure, universal fit on all major pipette brands. Available in filtered and non-filtered variants, they ensure precise, contamination-free liquid transfer for even the most sensitive applications.

  • Certified free of DNase, RNase, pyrogens, and human DNA
  • Filtered variants block aerosols to protect your pipette
  • Low-retention surface maximises sample recovery
  • Available racked, bulk, or in sterile packaging

Key Features

Engineered for Purity

Cleanroom Manufactured

Produced under strict cleanroom conditions to guarantee purity from production line to pipette.

Aerosol Barrier Filters

Filter tips trap aerosols and liquids before they reach the pipette shaft — essential for PCR and sensitive assays.

Universal Fit

Precision-moulded collars seat securely on AUTRIX and all major pipette brands with no wobble or leaks.

Low Retention

Hydrophobic tip surface releases the full sample volume, improving accuracy with viscous or precious liquids.
